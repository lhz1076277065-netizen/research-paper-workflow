# Literature Evidence Workflow

Use this workflow for literature discovery, claim support, novelty assessment and reference-list completion.

## Contents

- Search contract
- Rolling five-year evidence window
- WoS and JCR quality verification
- Top-article classification
- Reference breadth and quantity
- Evidence matrix and saturation
- Citation integrity
- Acceptance gate

## Search Contract

Define before searching:

- research questions, constructs, mechanisms, population or material, setting and outcomes;
- synonyms, controlled vocabulary, abbreviations and excluded meanings;
- databases, indexes, document types, languages and date of final search;
- inclusion, exclusion, screening and deduplication rules;
- evidence needed for background, theory, method, data, comparison, limitation and contradiction.

Use Web of Science Core Collection as a primary citation-discovery source when access is available. Add domain databases and official repositories when they improve coverage. Record exact queries, filters, result counts, export date and database coverage.

## Rolling Five-Year Evidence Window

Compute the recent window as the current calendar year and the preceding four years. Include early-access records where relevant and state the final index date.

- Prioritize recent primary research and authoritative reviews for current empirical, technical and methodological claims.
- Target at least 70% of nonfoundational journal references from the rolling five-year window and verified JCR Q1 or Q2 journals when the field provides sufficient eligible literature.
- If the target cannot be met, document the search evidence, field-specific scarcity and the reason older or lower-quartile sources are necessary.
- Retain older seminal theory, original methods, standards, landmark datasets and historically necessary evidence. Do not replace a primary foundational source with a recent paper that merely cites it.
- Do not use the five-year window to erase contradictory evidence or create false novelty.

The total reference count is determined by claim coverage and search saturation, not by padding to a fixed number.

## WoS and JCR Quality Verification

For every source classified as recent high-tier evidence, verify and record:

- Web of Science Core Collection index and accession or stable record information where available;
- journal title and ISSN;
- JCR year, subject category and quartile;
- the journal's quartile when multiple categories differ;
- article year, document type, DOI and correction or retraction status;
- access date for changing metadata.

Use JCR, not an unverified website or publisher claim, for Q1/Q2 classification. JCR is updated annually; never assume that a journal's current quartile was identical in every publication year. Mark unavailable or ambiguous quartile information as unverified.

## Top-Article Classification

Do not equate journal quartile with article quality. Classify a paper as a top candidate only after evaluating:

- direct relevance to the exact claim;
- methodological rigor and transparency;
- sample, data or benchmark quality;
- external validity and independent replication;
- citation influence normalized for field and publication age;
- recognized highly cited or hot-paper status only when verified;
- centrality to the research lineage;
- whether later evidence confirms, narrows or contradicts it.

Prefer the best primary source for a claim. Use reviews to map a field, then trace decisive claims to original studies.

## Reference Breadth and Quantity

Treat the reference list as insufficient when any required evidence class is empty, regardless of the nominal count.

- Cite at least one directly inspected primary or authoritative source for every core factual, theoretical and methodological claim.
- Use at least two independent sources for major contested, safety-relevant or generalizing claims when eligible evidence exists.
- Cover the closest recent competing methods, not only broad background papers.
- Cite original method, instrument, dataset, software, standard and benchmark sources where applicable.
- Support Discussion interpretations with both confirming and contradictory domain evidence.
- Distribute citations where claims occur; do not concentrate nearly all references in the Introduction.
- Build a screened candidate pool broader than the final bibliography, then retain sources by relevance, rigor and nonredundant evidentiary value.
- Compare reference breadth with the target article type and venue norms when available, without using citation padding to imitate a count.

The final count is adequate only when claim coverage, comparator coverage and thematic saturation all pass.

## Evidence Matrix and Saturation

Maintain:

`Reference | Claim supported | Evidence type | Population/setting | Method | Main finding | Limitation | Year | WoS index | JCR year/category/quartile | DOI | Citation influence | Status`

Coverage requirements:

- each core factual or methodological claim has a directly supporting source;
- major contested claims have multiple independent sources and contradictory evidence is represented;
- each proposed contribution is compared with the closest methods and most recent relevant work;
- theory, method, data source, benchmark and interpretation references are all covered;
- search iterations continue until new eligible results no longer change the conceptual themes, comparator set or claim boundaries.

## Citation Integrity

- Open and inspect the source; do not cite from titles, abstracts or secondary mentions alone when the full claim requires full-text verification.
- Verify author list, title, year, venue, volume, issue, pages or article number and DOI.
- Check corrections, expressions of concern, retractions and materially disputed findings.
- Match every in-text statement to what the source actually demonstrates.
- Do not cite a paper for a method, dataset or conclusion it only mentions.
- Do not inflate references with irrelevant Q1/Q2 articles.
- Keep citation language calibrated to study design; association is not causation and simulation is not field validation.

## Acceptance Gate

Pass only when the search log, screening record and evidence matrix exist; recent high-tier evidence meets the justified target or has a documented exception; all required evidence classes and core claims are covered; metadata and DOI checks pass; contradictory evidence is represented; and the reference list reaches conceptual and methodological saturation without padding.
