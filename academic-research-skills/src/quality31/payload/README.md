# Academic Research Skills v3.4.1

One orchestrator and eighteen standalone specialists generated from maintained source. Every professional step must invoke a matching pinned entry from the fourteen designated repositories, read and apply its workflow, and bind current inputs and outputs before handoff. There are 23 verified entrypoints; preparation alone is not professional completion. Missing matching sources block the step without host fallback.

Full research uses bounded feasibility (at most 45 minutes without a budget), then a single authorized total budget. Existing user budgets are preserved. Active steps are registered for recovery and cannot be omitted at stage exits. Sufficient existing evidence can move directly from design to writing with a recorded decision. Pause/resume preserves deadlines and token baselines. Focused writing and maintenance remain within scope.

[Start](START_HERE.en.md), [mandatory professional flow](docs/mandatory-professional-flow.md), [phase control](docs/phase-control.md), [capability routing](docs/capability-routing.md), [installation and rollback](INSTALLATION.zh-CN.md), [中文](README.zh-CN.md), [stable release](https://github.com/lhz1076277065-netizen/research-paper-workflow/releases/tag/v3.4.1).

Software checks cover identity and workflow behavior, not scientific validity. Native hardware, workbench and human verification limits remain explicit. The phase tool manages only checked actions and its own child processes; actual host accounting is required for token ceilings. Validation and historical integration evidence are in the source archive under evaluations/v3.4.1 and the preserved rc.1/rc.2 directories.
