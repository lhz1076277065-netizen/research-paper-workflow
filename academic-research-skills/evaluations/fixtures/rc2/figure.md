# Synthetic main-figure task
All results are frozen synthetic development fixtures, not observations from a research project.
Lower loss is better, in loss points. A and B were evaluated on the same independent clusters
within each condition and group. Clusters are independent between conditions.
The supplied intervals are 95% percentile cluster-bootstrap intervals from the paired evaluation:
A/B columns are marginal intervals; diff_low/high are intervals for the paired difference A−B.
Underlying cluster observations, aggregate bootstrap draws and cross-group covariances are unavailable.
The target weights were specified for each condition; they are not the empirical cluster proportions.
There are no causal assignments and no equivalence tests.

Make one main figure for a 180 mm wide by at most 105 mm high layout using an appropriate
professional implementation. Provide editable code, vector output, target-size preview and a caption.
Readers should be able to answer: does the target-weighted average advantage hold in both conditions;
which groups contradict it; what does the supplied uncertainty concern; and how much of the changed
average remains when C1 target weights are applied to C2 results?
Choose the scientific expression and inspect the actual final figure. Do not make a paper or acquire
new observations. Explain any remaining uncertainty in a short operation note.
