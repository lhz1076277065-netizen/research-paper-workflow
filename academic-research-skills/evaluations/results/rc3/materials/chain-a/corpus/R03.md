# R03 — fictional 2019 article, work W-B
§1 works with finite labelled, time-ordered DAGs and induced embeddings as in
R01. Its “interval-closed” image means: every directed path in G whose distinct
endpoints lie in the image of f has every vertex in that image.
§2 proves sufficiency for reachability equivalence: any target path between
image endpoints stays in the image; inducedness and injection give its inverse
path in H. Forward implication follows by mapping a source path.
§3 does not claim interval closure is necessary. Aliases: “path-closed induced
image”; “query-preserving export”. It gives no smallest certificate algorithm.
