# Fictional official policy snapshots as of 2026-09-30
These are complete closed-world policies, not real journal recommendations.
Journal Apex — scope includes quantitative comparisons. Research articles must
establish a novel general causal or predictive method and external validation.
Technical diagnostic notes and mere reanalyses are not accepted article types.
Journal Lens — diagnostic methods and measurement-comparison audience. Policy
v2, dated 2026-09-01: Technical Note, main text at most 2500 words; double-blind
review; separate title page; editable manuscript and figure caption; supporting
material and data/code access statement required. Restricted raw data with a
specific controlled-access statement is permitted. Funding/conflicts/author and
ethics declarations are required before actual submission, not assumed approved.
Journal Replica — scope accepts replication and boundary tests, but policy v1
requires openly deposited raw unit-level records and identifiers with no
controlled-access exception. Its topic fit does not override the fixture license.
An old Lens policy dated 2025 permitted an author line in the review manuscript;
that policy was superseded by v2 and is supplied only to test version selection.
