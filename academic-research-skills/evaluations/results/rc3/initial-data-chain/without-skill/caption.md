# SYNTHETIC — Figure 1

**Target composition, stratum disagreement, and the paired comparison.** (a) The stipulated target contains 80% g1 and 20% g2; the observed sample contains four independent units per stratum and therefore has a 50%/50% composition. Colors and hatches identify strata. (b) Small open circles (g1) and squares (g2) show all eight unit-level A−B differences after unit conversion, exact-event deduplication, and within-unit/method averaging of equally precise technical repetitions. Negative differences favor A because scores are lower-is-better. Diamonds show stratum means (−1.00 and +3.00 score), the target-weighted mean (−0.20), and the sample-mix mean (+1.00). Whiskers are **approximate 95% percentile bootstrap intervals**, not standard deviations or technical-repetition error bars: [−1.375, −0.625] for g1, [1.50, 5.00] for g2, [−0.65, 0.25] for the fixed target mix, and [0.1875, 2.00] for the sample mix. Each stratum resamples its four paired independent units with replacement, preserving A/B pairing; all 256 ordered resamples per stratum and all 65,536 joint combinations are enumerated. Weights remain fixed. The intervals are conditional on the fixture's stipulated independence and empirical within-stratum distributions; their population coverage and real-world applicability are unvalidated. No significance symbols or causal interpretation are used. The target interval crosses zero despite a negative point estimate. The figure is 180 mm × 100 mm; the SVG retains editable text and vector marks.
