# SYNTHETIC — Figure 1

**Composition changes the descriptive ranking of the same paired measurements.** (a) The stipulated target comprises 80% stratum 1 and 20% stratum 2, whereas the eight observed independent units comprise 50% of each stratum. Color and hatching identify strata. (b) Open circles and squares show each paired unit's mean A score minus its mean B score after event-copy removal, score conversion, date-valid metadata joining, and averaging technical repetitions. There are four independent units in each stratum; vertical offsets separate points without altering their values. Filled circles/squares show stratum means, and diamonds show composition-weighted means. All rows use the same score axis; negative values favor A because scores are lower-is-better. Stratum means are -1.00 and +3.00 score, the target mean is -0.20, and the sampled-composition mean is +1.00. Capped segments show **conditional 95% paired-unit percentile bootstrap intervals**, not SD or SEM: paired units are sampled with replacement within each stratum, A and B stay together, and weights are held fixed. All 256 ordered within-stratum draws and 65,536 joint configurations are enumerated; endpoints use the inverse empirical CDF at 2.5% and 97.5%. The target interval is [-0.65, +0.25], which includes zero; the sampled-composition interval is [+0.1875, +2.0000]. Figure annotations round values to two decimal places. Intervals condition on empirical strata and fixture-stipulated independence; they are not guaranteed 95% population coverage or causal intervals. No significance test or star annotation is used. Manufactured material supplies no real-world validation.

**Alt text:** Two composition bars show target 80/20 versus observed 50/50. A common-axis forest display shows four negative paired differences in stratum 1 and four positive differences in stratum 2. The target diamond lies at -0.20 with an interval crossing zero; the sampled-composition diamond lies at +1.00. Strata therefore disagree, and changing the composition reverses the point ranking.
