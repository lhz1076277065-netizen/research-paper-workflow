**Figure 1. Target-weighted loss differences and group-level exceptions in frozen synthetic development fixtures.** All values are synthetic summaries, not research observations. Loss is measured in loss points; negative A−B favors A and positive A−B favors B. **a**, Circles (C1) and squares (C2) show group-level paired differences, with the supplied 95% percentile cluster-bootstrap intervals for A−B. A and B were evaluated on the same independent clusters within each condition and group; clusters are independent between conditions. Cluster counts and prescribed target weights are shown alongside the contrasts. Target weights are not empirical cluster proportions. The supplied marginal intervals for A and B are not plotted or used to reconstruct paired uncertainty. **b**, Target-weighted differences, computed as the sum of target weight × group difference, are point estimates only. C1 favors B on average (+0.68), whereas C2 favors A (−0.84); an average advantage for A therefore does not hold in both conditions at the point-estimate level. C1 G2 and G3 run against its average direction, and C2 G1 runs against its average direction. C2 G3 has a negative point estimate, but its interval spans zero and remains compatible with both directions; this is not evidence of equivalence. The hollow C2 square applies C1 weights (G1/G2/G3: 50/20/30%) to C2 results, yielding −0.12. Relative to C1, this fixed-weight change is −0.80, retaining 52.6% (rounded to 53% in the figure) of the original −1.52 change. This is a descriptive sensitivity comparison, not a causal decomposition. Intervals for weighted means, changes, and the retained fraction cannot be obtained from the supplied summaries: underlying cluster observations, aggregate bootstrap draws, and cross-group covariances are unavailable. No equivalence or causal tests were supplied or performed.
