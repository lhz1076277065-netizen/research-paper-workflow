**Figure 1. Method ranking depends on stratum and weighting in fixed synthetic results.** (a,b) Supplied losses for methods A (blue circles) and B (orange squares) in the reference and changed acquisition conditions. Horizontal whiskers reproduce each row's supplied 95% interval without re-estimation; vertical offsets aid visibility. Labels give the supplied per-method stratum counts. Loss units were not specified. (c) The observed S1:S2 composition changes from 80:20 to 20:80, with total n=100 per condition per method. (d) Filled diamonds show observed-weight differences, B−A: −0.44 in the reference condition (A=4.80, B=4.36) and +0.92 in the changed condition (A=6.60, B=7.52). The open diamond applies the reference 80:20 weights to changed-condition losses as a descriptive sensitivity comparison (A=5.40, B=5.48; difference +0.08). Negative values favor B. B has lower loss in S1 and higher loss in S2 in both conditions; the observed overall ranking reverses. The unqualified claim “B is a better estimator overall” is therefore unsupported by these results. Aggregate and difference intervals were not supplied and are not plotted. No significance tests, causal identification, or mechanism evidence are available.
