# Evidence data sources

NYC indicator JSON snapshots are from the NYC Department of Health and Mental Hygiene EHDP-data repository, commit d3558441d122b4db4b9fd4d2011be500df6b584a. Original source URLs, timestamps and SHA256 are in study/download-log.json. Original Apache 2.0 LICENSE is preserved in study/raw/LICENSE and method-input/LICENSE. These are published district estimates and spatial summaries, not confidential household records.

The NOAA NCEI Global Hourly snapshot broad-A/phx-2025.csv is the unchanged payload downloaded from https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/data/global-hourly/access/2025/72278023183.csv . The actual downloaded file ends 2025-08-26; it is not claimed to be a complete annual archive. The July Sunday subset and calculations are research derivatives. SHA256 and retrieval reading scope are in broad-A/operations.json and downloads.json. NOAA NCEI open-data policy: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/sites/default/files/2023-12/NCEI%20PD-10-2-02%20-%20Open%20Data%20Policy%20Signed.pdf . Data are attributed to NOAA; inclusion implies no endorsement.

The NYC announced schedule CSV is a research transcription of the cited public announcement, not a verified attendance/open-door record. Other CSV probability tables and service schedules labeled hypothetical/synthetic are constructed research evidence, not observed households or interventions.

Third-party full papers, figures and upstream tool code remain outside this delivery. Reading logs preserve citations, exact reading scope and available download hashes. The repository MIT license does not replace external data terms.
