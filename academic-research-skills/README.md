# Academic Research Skills v3.5.0

One orchestrator and eighteen standalone specialists use an evidence-revisable scientific argument throughout research: evidenced gap, prior limitations or competing explanations, contribution with discriminating evidence, and new knowledge with boundaries. The current route board connects topic choice, experiment decisions, figures and manuscript claims. A hypothesis precedes testing; conclusions follow results. This is the suite's framework, not a universal journal acceptance formula.

Every professional step still invokes and applies a matching pinned entry from the fourteen designated repositories before finish/check handoff. All 23 entries remain indexed. Full research supplies its current route board to later steps; missing sources block the affected step without host fallback. Focused work stays within the requested scope.

Sufficient evidence leads to writing; no feasible decisive test leads to a report. Negative results can revise the story and retain meaningful contributions.
